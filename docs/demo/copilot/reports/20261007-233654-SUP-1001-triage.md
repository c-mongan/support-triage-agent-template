## 🗺️ How It Works (and Where It Breaks)
The SDK batches events and flushes on a timer, at 20 events, and when the page is hidden (doc row 3). In 3.12.0 the unload flush became a keepalive `fetch` on `visibilitychange`. Per BEACON-142, Safari cancels those requests when they begin during a route change or redirect, so they never reach ingestion and nothing is logged. A confirmation-page event fired just before navigation would be lost, whereas earlier events on the page still arrive.
